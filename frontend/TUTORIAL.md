# NaildBySteph System Tutorial: Mastering Your Salon Hub

Welcome to the fully upgraded NaildBySteph management system! This tutorial will guide you through all the powerful new features designed to streamline your business, from AI-powered insights to advanced inventory and rental management.

---

## 1. Salon AI: Your Digital Homegirl Assistant

The Salon AI is your personal, always-on assistant, ready to provide daily briefings and answer business-related questions with a unique, supportive personality.

### How to Use the Chatbot
1.  **Access**: Click the floating **pink bubble** (or the AI icon in the Admin panel) to open the chat interface.
2.  **Ask**: Type any business-related question (e.g., "What's my schedule look like?", "How do I handle a no-show?")
3.  **Personality**: The AI is configured with an "older homegirl from Sanger" persona, using a Valley Girl x Chicana cadence.

### Admin Configuration (Admin Panel > Salon AI)
*   **Tone**: Adjust the AI's personality (e.g., more professional, more casual).
*   **Briefing Style**: Choose between a short summary or a long, detailed daily briefing.
*   **Reminder Timing**: Set when you want to receive your daily briefing.

---

## 2. VIP Whitelist & Loyalty Program

Reward your best clients and encourage repeat business with the integrated loyalty system.

### VIP Whitelist (Admin Panel > VIP & Blocked Dates)
*   **Adding VIPs**: Manually add a client's email to the VIP Whitelist. VIPs may receive priority booking access or special promotions.
*   **VIP Scores**: The system automatically tracks a client's **VIP Score** and **Lifetime Spend** based on their booking history.

### Loyalty Stamps
*   **Tracking**: The system automatically tracks loyalty stamps for clients.
*   **Management**: You can manually add or remove stamps in the Admin panel if needed.

---

## 3. Nail Appointment Simulator

The Simulator is a powerful training tool for new techniques or practicing complex services.

### How to Access and Use
1.  **Access**: Navigate to the `/simulator` route (or a dedicated link on the main site).
2.  **Workflow**: Follow the steps of a nail appointment (Clean, Push Cuticle, Clip, Base Coat, Color, Top Coat).
3.  **AI Guidance**: The Salon AI provides **real-time feedback** after each step, offering tips and corrections in its signature style.
4.  **Scoring**: Receive a final score based on the quality of your work, encouraging perfection.

---

## 4. Inventory Tracking & Automation

Keep track of your supplies and never run out of essential products again.

### Inventory Management (Admin Panel > Inventory)
*   **Update Stock**: Manually adjust the quantity of any item (e.g., "Monomer," "Top Coat").
*   **Restock Threshold**: Set a minimum quantity for each item. When stock drops below this level, the system flags it.

### Automated Usage Tracking
*   **Deduction**: When you mark an appointment as **Completed**, the system automatically deducts the necessary supplies from your inventory based on the service performed.

### AI Restock Alerts
*   **Daily Briefing**: The Salon AI will include a **Restock Alert** in your daily briefing if any item is below its set threshold.

---

## 5. Chair/Workspace Rental System

Expand your business by renting out your spare chairs or stations to other independent nail technicians.

### Admin Management (Admin Panel > Chair Rental)
*   **Space Management**: Add, edit, and set the **Hourly Rate** for each rental space (e.g., "Chair 1," "Pedicure Station").
*   **Booking Review**: View all past and upcoming rental bookings, including payment status.

### Tech Booking (Dedicated `/tech-booking` Page)
*   **Booking**: Other nail techs can visit this page to select a space, choose a date/time, and see the calculated total cost based on your hourly rate.
*   **Payment**: The system tracks the payment status of each booking.

---

## 6. PWA Conversion (Mobile App Installation)

The entire system is now a Progressive Web App (PWA), meaning you can install it directly onto your phone or tablet like a native app.

### Installation Instructions
1.  **Open in Browser**: Open the NaildBySteph website in a modern mobile browser (Chrome, Safari).
2.  **Install Prompt**: Look for the **Install App** prompt (often a banner or a small icon in the address bar).
3.  **Follow Steps**: Follow the browser's instructions to "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App."
4.  **Launch**: The app will now launch in a full-screen, dedicated window without the browser's address bar.
